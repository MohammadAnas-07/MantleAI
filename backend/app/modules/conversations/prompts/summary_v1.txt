Always keep, as short bullet points under these headings:
- Decisions: every decision made and WHY it was made.
- Constraints and preferences: everything the user stated they want, need or dislike.
- Files and code: file names, paths, functions, commands and versions mentioned.
- Errors: exact error messages and what was done about them.
- Open questions: anything still undecided or waiting for an answer.
